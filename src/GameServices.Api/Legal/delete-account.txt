# Delete your Steplings account

## In the game

Go to Options, then Account & server, then Delete my account, and type your password. Your account is deleted straight away, and your progress is removed from your phone.

## If you can't get into the game

Email [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] from the email address on your account, with your username, and ask us to delete it. We will do it within a month.

## What is deleted

Your account, monsters, Glimmer, steps, friends, guild membership and messages. If you were using the same account in another of our games, it is deleted there too.

Any Glimmer that other players had put on your monsters goes back to them. A report about a player is kept for up to a year after we have finished looking into it, so that we can keep players safe. Copies in our backups are replaced within about 30 days.
